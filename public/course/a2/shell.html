Learning mode
Self-StudyYou work alone. Explanations are fuller, there is extra practice, every wrong answer tells you why, and the last stage shows you which stages still need work.
1-to-1Your teacher is your speaking partner. The explanation is lighter because the teacher fills the gaps aloud, and feedback comes after each speaking task.
Group StudySpeaking happens in pairs with role cards and a swap. The teacher gives short feedback after each task — one thing done well, one to try next time.
